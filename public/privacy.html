⚡ Pace Pilot

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

Pace Pilot ("the app") is a personal productivity tool that connects to your Google account to display your tasks, calendar events, email, and Drive files in one place. This policy explains what data is accessed, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Access

When you sign in with Google, the app requests access to the following Google services:

  • Google Calendar — to read and display your events.
  • Google Tasks — to read, create, update, and delete your task lists and tasks.
  • Gmail — to read and label your emails within the app.
  • Google Drive — to browse and display your files (read-only).

2. How We Use Your Data

All data fetched from Google APIs is used solely to display and manage your personal information within the Pace Pilot interface. We do not:

  • Sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.
  • Use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • Store your Google data on our servers — data is fetched in real time and held only in your browser session.

3. Data Storage

Account credentials and preferences are stored securely via Appwrite, an open-source backend platform. No Google content (emails, files, events, or tasks) is persisted outside your browser.

4. Google API Disclosure

Pace Pilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Security

Access tokens are managed by Appwrite's OAuth2 session layer and are never exposed to third parties. All communication with Google APIs occurs over HTTPS.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke Pace Pilot's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions. Revoking access removes all ability for the app to read or modify your Google data.
